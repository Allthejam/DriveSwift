# **App Name**: DriveSwift

## Core Features:

- Smart Diary Management: Display a calendar interface for instructors to manage their availability and schedule lessons.
- Comprehensive Pupil Records: Allow instructors to maintain records of their students, including contact information, progress, and lesson history.
- AI-Powered Lesson Planner: A tool that helps instructors create personalized learning plans for students based on their skill level and learning pace, enhancing lesson effectiveness.
- Intuitive Progress Tracking: Provide a progress-tracking feature where instructors can record the development of their students.
- Integrated Training Aids: Enable instructors to access integrated training aids, enhancing their teaching capabilities.
- Pupil Progress App: Provide the ability for students to log in to view their progress and schedule

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant blue (#3B82F6) to reflect trust, confidence, and professionalism, fitting for the educational context.
- Background color: A light blue (#F0F9FF), nearly desaturated, providing a calm and clean backdrop that's easy on the eyes.
- Accent color: A slightly more saturated indigo (#4F46E5) to complement the primary blue and add visual interest to key interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) provides a clean and modern appearance, and good readability.
- Use modern, minimalist icons to represent different features.
- Employ a clean and intuitive layout, optimizing for usability.
- Use subtle animations for feedback.
